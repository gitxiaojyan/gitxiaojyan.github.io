Our Story

18 Years of Streetwear Craftsmanship

Founded in 2008 in Humen, Dongguan — the heart of China's garment industry — Dongguan Yixian Clothing started as a small sewing workshop with 20 workers and a single mission: make apparel that streetwear brands are proud to put their label on.

Today, we operate a 2,000 square meter vertically integrated facility with over 50 skilled workers, serving more than 200 brands across 30+ countries. From heavyweight hoodies and graphic tees to acid wash sweatpants and rhinestone jacket, our clients trust us to deliver consistent quality at competitive prices.

What sets us apart is our complete in-house capability — fabric development, pattern making, sampling, cutting, sewing, washing, printing and embroidery all under one roof. This means faster turnaround, tighter quality control, and true customization at every stage for both men's and women's collections.
